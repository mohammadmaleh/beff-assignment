COMPANY OVERVIEW

This company is an innovative technology business operating at the intersection of advanced battery technology and software development. It supports customers throughout the battery lifecycle, from research and development to production readiness and data-driven optimization.

The Germany-based team serves as the company's software and digital solutions hub, developing platforms that enable customers to analyze, manage, and extract valuable insights from complex battery data. By combining expertise in battery science with modern software engineering, the company helps customers accelerate innovation, improve product performance, and make informed technical and business decisions.

With customers across Europe, North America, and Asia, this company is experiencing continued growth driven by the increasing demand for battery technologies, electrification, and data-driven development solutions.
